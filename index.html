07 · Questions

Good questions. Straight answers.

What happens after I send my footage?

You send your raw files and tell us what the video should do. You get a first cut within 48 hours. You send notes, and we keep refining until you are happy to publish.

How fast will I get my first cut?

Within 48 hours of receiving your footage and a short brief. Need it faster? Ask on the call and we will tell you what is possible.

How much does it cost?

Every project is different, so we do not list prices. After a short call about your goals and footage, you get a clear quote. The sample edit is free.

What is in the free sample?

A short edit made from a clip of your own footage. Send your channel link and we reply within 24 hours.

How many changes can I ask for?

Send your notes on the first cut and we keep refining until you are happy to publish.

I only need one video. Is that fine?

Yes. Start with the Explore plan. If you later want help with your channel, we can add that separately.

Can I get long and short videos together?

Yes. We can mix them in one project.

Can you run my whole channel?

Yes. Starter covers the day-to-day. Pro adds a growth plan. Done for you adds making the videos too.

Who will I talk to?

The founders, Nishant and Ayush. No handoffs.
