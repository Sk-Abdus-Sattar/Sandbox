What goes into the work

    Editing paced to keep viewers watching.

02 · The approach

What full-service YouTube management looks like

A channel is a system, not a run of lucky uploads. The retainer gives you a roadmap tied to your business goals, a production line on a fixed cadence and a feedback loop from analytics into future briefs.

We treat thumbnails and titles as a product line: rounds of fresh ideas, studies of competitors, tests of two versions and swaps after publishing for videos that underperform.

03 · Included

How it differs from hiring an editor

  • An editor executes cuts. A channel manager owns growth outcomes: packaging, cadence, finding out why viewers stop watching, and the roadmap.

Questions

Good questions. Clear answers.

No fine print. Just what you need to know before we start.

What does YouTube channel management include?

Strategy, planning, scripting support, editing, thumbnails, titles, search words, publishing, a plan for replying to your community and monthly analytics. You only record.

How is this different from hiring an editor?

An editor executes cuts. A channel manager owns growth outcomes, including packaging, cadence, finding out why viewers stop watching, and the roadmap.

How long until I see growth?

Growth depends on your audience, your content and your consistency. We make no timeline or subscriber promises.

Do you handle thumbnails and titles?

Yes, scoped within your channel plan.

Who owns the channel and the files?

You do. Work happens in your own accounts (Google, Frame.io, Notion), and you keep every asset if the relationship ends.

What are the channel plans?

Starter covers channel operations, Pro adds strategy, and Done-For-You includes production. Every plan has a dedicated channel manager.

How is my project quoted?

Every project is scoped individually. We share a clear quote on your discovery call.
