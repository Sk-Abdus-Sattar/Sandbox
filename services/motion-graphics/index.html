02 · The approach

Motion design as a growth lever, not a decoration

Motion can condense a complex idea into seconds, and it signals production quality before a viewer consciously notices.

Intentional motion clarifies an idea and gives your opening a distinct identity. A well-branded lower third can make a talking-head video feel like a recognizable show. We treat motion as strategy.

Questions

Good questions. Clear answers.

No fine print. Just what you need to know before we start.

What kinds of motion graphics do you create?

Moving type, logo animations and stings, explainers, app and website motion mockups, on-screen data visualization, animated lower thirds, brand transitions and cinematic visual effects.

Which tools do you use?

After Effects is the primary tool, with Cinema 4D and Element 3D for 3D work. You receive a rendered file, or a project file if you need editable source.

Can you add motion graphics to a video edited elsewhere?

Yes. We can add logo animations, moving type, overlays, callouts and transitions to footage edited by someone else.

Do you include concept and storyboarding?

Yes. Every project starts with a creative brief. Anything over 15 seconds also gets a style frame and storyboard round, so the look is approved before any animation is built.

How long does a motion project take?

We agree scope and schedule on the discovery call and confirm milestones before work starts.

How much does it cost?

Every project is individually scoped. We share a clear quote on your discovery call.

What’s in the free sample edit?

A short edit using a clip from your footage, so you can judge the pacing, motion and finish before committing. Share your channel link and we’ll reply within 24 hours to arrange the sample.
