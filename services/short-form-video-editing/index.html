02 · The approach

Why most short-form fails in the first three seconds

Short-form depends on the hook. If the opening doesn’t hold viewers, the platform’s algorithm never gets a chance to distribute the video.

We build edits backward from the hook: on-screen text, framing, an audio spike and a pattern break first, then a middle and an ending shaped to keep the story moving.

Questions

Good questions. Clear answers.

No fine print. Just what you need to know before we start.

How much does short-form editing cost?

Pricing is scoped to your goals and the clips you need. We share a clear quote on the discovery call.

Do you repurpose long-form videos?

Yes, as an add-on. We turn podcasts, keynotes and long-form YouTube videos into 30–60 second vertical clips, choosing the highest-hook moments for each platform.

What’s included in a short-form edit?

Hook engineering, dynamic captions, sound design, sound effects, jump cuts, zooms, reframes, on-brand typography and platform-specific exports.

How fast is the turnaround?

The first cut arrives within 48 hours of receiving footage and a brief. We agree a schedule before a batch begins.

Do you write hooks and captions?

Yes. The on-screen hook and caption timing are shaped around your message, and we can scope alternate versions when useful.

Can I mix long-form and short-form?

Yes. Combine long-form editing with vertical clips and motion graphics in one engagement.

What’s in the free sample edit?

A short edit using a clip from your footage, so you can judge the pacing, motion and finish before committing. Share your channel link and we’ll reply within 24 hours to arrange the sample.
