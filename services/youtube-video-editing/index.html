02 · The approach

Long-form editing that keeps people watching

On YouTube, the edit is the product. A strong topic or a great camera can’t rescue a weak hook or a sagging middle.

The goal is watch time. Cuts, sound cues and motion elements all work to keep people watching, and the direction comes from your references and footage: a coherent story, not a pile of effects.

03 · Included

Included with every video

  • Rough cut, fine cut and final delivery
  • Extra footage (b-roll) research and placement

Questions

Good questions. Clear answers.

No fine print. Just what you need to know before we start.

How much does long-form YouTube editing cost?

Every project is individually scoped. We share a clear quote on your discovery call, based on your goals, footage and creative direction.

How fast is the turnaround?

Your first cut arrives within 48 hours of receiving your footage and brief. We agree on the scope upfront, and rush options can be discussed on the call.

Which kinds of channels do you edit for?

Commentary, education, business, tech reviews, vlogs, documentary, faceless channels and gaming.

Can you match my channel’s style?

Yes. We build a style guide from your best-performing videos, covering pacing, sound, motion and typography.

Do you make thumbnails and titles?

They’re included in Channel Management plans and available as add-ons for editing-only clients.

How do revisions work?

Give us clear feedback on the first cut and we’ll refine the edit until you’re happy. The brief keeps us aligned on direction throughout.

What if I only need one video?

Start with Explore for a single edit, with no long-term commitment.

What’s in the free sample edit?

A short edit using a clip from your footage, so you can judge the pacing, motion and finish before committing. Share your channel link and we’ll reply within 24 hours to arrange the sample.
